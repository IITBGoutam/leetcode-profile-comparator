LeetCode won't reveal a user's complete solved list publicly. Without one of the options below, that person is compared in approximate "public" mode (exact counts + only their last 20 solved). Two ways to get an exact list:
A · Import a shared export — easiest, and the only friendly option for your friend (no cookie). Open the share page → send it to your friend, then import the .json they send back.
B · Paste a session cookie — quickest for yourself. Your LEETCODE_SESSION (DevTools → Application → Cookies → leetcode.com). Sent only to this local server and, with Remember ticked, saved encrypted on this computer — never in the browser.
