Export your LeetCode solved list

A friend wants to compare progress with you. This grabs the list of problems you've solved — nothing else. No password, no cookie, no account access. It runs in your browser and saves a small file you send back.

1

Log in to LeetCode

Open leetcode.com and make sure you're signed in. Keep that tab open.

2

Run the exporter — pick either way

Easiest: drag this button up to your bookmarks bar. Then, while on a LeetCode tab, click the bookmark.

3

Send the file back

It downloads leetcode-solved-<you>.json (a list of problem slugs — safe to share). Send that file to your friend. They import it and the comparison is exact. That's it 🎉

Why this and not a cookie? Your LEETCODE_SESSION cookie is your login — sharing it is risky, and browsers hide it from scripts anyway. This exports only your public solved-problem slugs, so there's nothing sensitive to leak.
